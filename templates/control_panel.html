Create a Seller
Register a seller account. The system starts monitoring trust from this point.

3
Add a Product
List a product under the seller. Upload an image — the AI will compare it against complaint evidence.
4

File a complaint with an evidence image. The AI runs in the background and computes severity automatically.
5

Check the seller's real-time trust score, risk level, and the full feature vector that drove the decision.
